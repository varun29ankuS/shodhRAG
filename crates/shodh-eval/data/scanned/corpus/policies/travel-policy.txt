Travel and Expense Policy (Northwind Analytics)
Version 3.2, effective 1 January 2026.

Domestic travel per diem is INR 3,500 per day.
International travel per diem is USD 75 per day.
Flights under six hours are booked in economy class.
All travel must be approved in advance by the department head.
Expense claims must be filed within 30 days of the trip ending.
