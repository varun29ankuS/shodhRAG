# OCR error rates by scan resolution

We scanned 60 printed invoices at three resolutions and measured the character error rate (CER).

| Resolution (dpi) | CER | Pages per minute |
|---|---|---|
| 150 | 4.2% | 38 |
| 200 | 2.3% | 31 |
| 300 | 1.1% | 22 |

## Recommendation

Scan invoices at 300 dpi: the error rate drops below 1.5% while throughput stays above 20 pages per minute.
